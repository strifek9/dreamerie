# Version 4: harder dreams

Status: preproduction on `feature/v4-harder-dreams`. The owner requested a full audit of the 365 daily and 18 holiday cards, selective enrichment of sparse scenes, and harder and less predictable differences. On October 2 the owner separately authorized a work-in-progress commit and push as a checkpoint, not a V4 release or deployment. Version 3 remains on `main` and live.

## Inventory and direction

On September 30, 2026, the current catalogue had 383 cards and exactly 1,915 authored clues. A wording audit found 626 color, 665 disappearance, 335 shape, 182 pattern, and only 3 relational clues. These categories overlap and are not difficulty measurements, but 322 cards have at least four clues in the color/disappearance/shape categories. This supports the owner's feedback that the puzzle grammar has become predictable.

The target mix to test is one easy, one medium, one hard, and two extremely hard clues per card. Difficulty must come from observation and changed relationships, actions, order, mechanisms, reflections, or meaningful subtle colors. Do not make clues microscopic, ambiguous, buried in noise, or dependent on guessing a story interpretation. Reversing an established pattern can work when both orders are legible. Near-hue changes (red/orange, blue/green, yellow/brown) can diversify the middle of the mix, but five near-hue changes would be another predictable pattern. Spread five nonoverlapping targets across the scene and verify each at phone game size and with zoom.

The visible pending circle and collision radius stay at the current 8% artwork width, with any overlap counting as a hit. The game still has two minutes, five confirmed guesses, unpausable timing, accuracy-first results and browser-local saves. Difficulty is editorial and hidden from players. Two extreme clues in a two-minute round may be too punishing; use actual player completion and frustration data before accepting the mix collection-wide.

Scenes that are genuinely thin may gain a few connected happenings and a more active impossible premise. Do not equate edge density with quality, cover all negative space, or repeat the same motifs. Original-only enrichment candidates were generated for cards 002, 074, 133 and 237 and are saved in the local Dreamerie ChatGPT project workspace under `artwork-review/v4-preproduction-20260930/`; card 173 was subsequently enriched with drifting jigsaw corners and a key shadow. An edge-density ranking of all 383 originals only nominated calm candidates for human inspection.
